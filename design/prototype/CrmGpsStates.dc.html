Roofing CRM · GPS and pin states

CRM · not Task A

“Use my location” is a map control and, at 390, the Locate chip in the sticky header. Every state appears in the same chip at the top of the map, so the radius, filters and list stay usable. Drop pin: click the map or drag the pin (1440, 768); at 390 a fixed crosshair at the map centre with Place pin here, and a long-press also drops it. The pin label shows its latitude, longitude and the nearest gazetteer place. Sample positions; accuracy figures are examples.
