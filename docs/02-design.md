**Signature moments in this app's brief:** #1 live radius ring (D-2), #2 roof-age heat (D-1, D-3), #3 triage cards (D-4, D-6), #4 "Why is this a lead?" chips (D-4), #5 agent → map handoff with editable assumption chips (D-7), #8 "View lineage in Explorer" (D-5). All six are clickable in the prototype. The § 15 app switcher is drawn as a menu (`CrmAppsMenu`, E-94) after round CRM-R1.

**Decisions (PO, 2026-10-21):**
- The app uses the guide's `assessment-guide/design-system/tokens.css`, not the prototype copy (#21).
- Task A's app is named "CountyLens", as it ships at `countylens.aldarraj.com` (#24).
- The first build ships the NoAddress and NoContractor boards; the address and "as printed" boards are the state shown once contract v1.1 fields reach the manifest (`design/docs/CRM-HANDOFF.md` §3, round CRM-R2).
- Address and contractor name come from a Task A follow-up (FU-A-01 in the Task A repo, `docs/decisions/`; EXT-11, EXT-12). Until then the CRM shows the fallback states (#1, #2).
