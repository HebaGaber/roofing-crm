## Draft acceptance criteria (put these in the story file, then edit)

As a **roofing sales rep**, I want **to see what the CRM will do next**, so that **the product direction is visible**.

1. Given the navigation, when shown, then Campaigns, Estimates, Jobs, Invoices and Outreach are visible and disabled with a lock icon.
2. Given a disabled item, when hovered or focused, then the tooltip says coming soon.
3. Given a screen reader, when it reaches a disabled item, then it is announced as disabled (aria-disabled).
